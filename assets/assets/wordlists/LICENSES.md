# Word list

`eff_large_wordlist.txt` is the EFF long word list (7,776 words) by the
Electronic Frontier Foundation, https://www.eff.org/dice, used under the
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/). The dice numbers are
removed; the words are unchanged. ArDrive picks words from it to suggest a
strong password for a new account.
